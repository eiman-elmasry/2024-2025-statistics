♿ Accessibility

This dashboard is designed to be usable by people with visual impairments, following the WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines.

  • Strong contrast. All colours meet at least 4.5:1 contrast with the background, in light and dark mode, and numbers on coloured cells switch to black or white for legibility.
  • Colour-blind friendly. The palette, built around the Centre’s blue (#284B96), stays distinct for common types of colour blindness, and charts never rely on colour alone: values are written on the charts, and trend lines also differ by dash pattern and marker shape.
  • Screen readers. Every chart has a spoken description that reads out its values, and tabs, buttons and tables are labelled for assistive technology.
  • Larger text. The “Larger text” button at the top enlarges all text, including chart labels; text is never smaller than 13px.
  • Keyboard. Everything can be reached with the Tab key, with a clear focus outline; arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs.
  • Comfort. Large click targets, and no animation for anyone whose device is set to reduce motion.

Known limit: pop-up details on hover need a mouse or touch screen; the same values are available through the chart descriptions and the tables.

Definitions and notes from the source tables
Participant-days
For each activity, the number of participants multiplied by the actual number of days of training.
Blended training
Since 2024, blended participants are counted with face-to-face training, not with distance learners.
Distance learning
Tutor-based and self-guided online courses, communities of practice, WhatsApp microlearning and Udemy masterclasses.
Share of women
Worked out over participants whose gender is known; gender and region are not recorded for Udemy masterclasses or WhatsApp microlearning.
ICS
International civil servants, counted as a group of their own rather than by country.
Affiliation
Employer, worker and labour ministry affiliation is self-declared in self-guided courses and communities of practice.
